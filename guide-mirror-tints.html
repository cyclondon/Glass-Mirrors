Mirror Tints & Guardian Glass: A Complete Guide

Our featured mirror is Guardian Glass, made in Thailand — a globally recognised glass manufacturer known for consistent silvering quality, which means a cleaner reflection and better long-term resistance to black-spot corrosion than budget mirror glass. We also stock a range of standard and coloured mirror tints for different looks and budgets.

Silver Mirror — our standard mirror, the true reflection most people picture when they think "mirror." Brightest and most neutral of the range, and the most versatile for bathrooms, wardrobes, and general use. Available from 3mm to 6mm.

Guardian Glass Mirror (Thailand) — our featured premium option at 6mm, from Guardian Glass's Thailand facility. Same bright, neutral reflection as our standard Silver Mirror, with the added quality consistency of a globally recognised manufacturer — worth the choice when long-term clarity and corrosion resistance matter most.

Grey Mirror — a soft, smoky tint that slightly dims the reflection. Popular for feature walls and larger installations where a fully bright silver mirror would feel too reflective or clinical.

Bronze Mirror — a warm, amber-tinted reflection. Common in bar counters, wardrobe doors and accent panels where a warmer, more traditional look is wanted.

Gold Mirror — a rich, warm gold-tinted reflection. Used for statement feature pieces and higher-end interiors where a distinctive warm tone is wanted.

Which tint should you choose?

For everyday bathroom or dressing mirrors, Silver or the Guardian Glass Thailand mirror are the right call — both give the brightest, most accurate reflection and are easiest to match with existing fixtures. Grey and Bronze suit feature installations where you want the mirror itself to contribute to the room's tone rather than disappear into the wall. Gold is best reserved for standout accent pieces.
