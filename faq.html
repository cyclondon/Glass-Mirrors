FAQ

Frequently asked questions about ordering custom glass and mirrors.

How long has 升威 LE-RELAX been in business?

升威 LE-RELAX has been fabricating custom glass and aluminium products in Hong Kong since 1986. Our mirror line, 快靚鏡 Fai Leng Geng, uses Guardian Glass made in Thailand as standard, and our online configurator gives you a real price estimate in seconds, without needing to visit a showroom first.

Do you also make shower screens, windows and doors?

Yes. 升威 LE-RELAX is a comprehensive aluminium and glass fabrication company. Alongside glass and mirrors, we make custom shower screens and doors — see le-relaxshower.com — as well as aluminium windows and architectural glass for residential and commercial projects.

Do you serve all areas of Hong Kong?

Yes — our service area covers the whole of Hong Kong. Visit any of our three showrooms in Tai Kok Tsui, Tuen Mun or Fo Tan to view samples, or use the online configurator for an instant price estimate from anywhere. See photos, addresses and a map for each showroom on our Our Stores page.

Can I view physical mirror samples before ordering?

Yes. We encourage you to visit any of our three showrooms to see Guardian Glass tint samples and edge finishes in person before finalising your order.

What are your showroom opening hours?

Our showrooms in Tai Kok Tsui, Tuen Mun, and Fo Tan are open Monday–Friday 9:00am–5:30pm and Saturday 9:00am–3:00pm (closed Sunday). See our Our Stores page for directions to each.

How much does a custom mirror cost?

Price depends on shape, size, tint, thickness, and edge finish. Use the online configurator for an exact estimate based on your own measurements and choices — a small minimum billable area applies per panel (2 to 3 sq ft depending on shape), with volume discounts at 5+ and 10+ units.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes — our online configurator gives you a free, instant price estimate for any shape and size. Three WhatsApp buttons, one per showroom, stay visible as you configure and update automatically — just tap whichever store you'd like to send your spec to. See our first-time guide for a full walkthrough.

Can I get a custom size or shape?

Yes — the configurator supports rectangle, circle, oval, and triangle shapes at any size within the tool's range. If you need a shape not listed, contact any of our showrooms or send us a photo via WhatsApp and we'll let you know if we can fabricate it.

What mirror tints do you offer?

Silver, Guardian Glass (Thailand), Grey, Bronze, and Gold. See our mirror tints guide for what each looks like and where it's typically used.

What edge finish should I choose?

It depends on whether the edge will be visible. Raw or Flat Seamed suit mirrors hidden inside a frame; Polished is the standard for frameless mirrors; Beveled is a premium decorative option. See our edge finishes guide for the full breakdown.

How do I measure for my mirror?

Measure width and height (or diameter, for a circle) at the widest points of the space you're filling. If you're mounting inside a frame or recess, measure the opening rather than the wall space. A rough estimate is fine to get a starting price — our showroom team can help confirm exact measurements before we cut.

What thickness should I choose?

Larger mirrors generally benefit from extra thickness for rigidity and to reduce visible flex or waviness in the reflection. Smaller mirrors can use a thinner, lighter option. Our showroom team can advise on the right thickness for your specific size on request.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, bank transfers, FPS (Faster Payment System), and cash at any of our three Hong Kong showrooms. Payment details are confirmed directly with our staff once your order is placed.

What is your turnaround time for custom orders?

Because we handle our own fabrication, standard custom mirror orders are typically ready for pickup within a week, depending on tint, size, and finish. Contact your nearest showroom for current schedule estimates.

Can I modify my order after it's been submitted?

Because fabrication begins quickly after confirmation, changes can only be accommodated if production hasn't started yet. If you need to adjust dimensions or finish, contact us via phone or WhatsApp immediately using the details below.

Is installation included?

We supply and fabricate mirrors to your exact measurements, ready for fitting — installation isn't something we provide directly, but we're happy to recommend trusted installers. Ask in-store or via WhatsApp.

What happens if my mirror arrives damaged or has a defect?

Inspect all glass upon receipt or collection. If you discover any transit damage, scratching, or silvering defect, notify us immediately via WhatsApp with clear photos before attempting installation, and we'll arrange a prompt replacement or adjustment for any verified issue.

How do I clean and maintain my mirror?

Clean with a soft cloth and a mild glass cleaner, and avoid spraying cleaner directly onto the edges, where moisture can eventually reach the silvering and cause black spots over time. Avoid abrasive pads or ammonia-heavy chemicals.

Do you handle commercial or large-scale wholesale projects?

Yes. In addition to retail orders for homeowners, our parent operations fabricate and supply mirrors, glass, and aluminium products at scale for developers, contractors, and renovation projects across Hong Kong. Reach out to our team to discuss bulk requirements.
