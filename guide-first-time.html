First Time Here? The Complete Guide to Using Our Configurator

Never used an online glass or mirror configurator before? This walks through the whole process, start to finish — you'll have a real price estimate in under a minute, whether you're configuring a glass panel or a mirror.

1. Choose a shape

Pick from rectangle, circle, oval, or triangle at the top of the configurator. This decides which dimension fields you'll fill in next — a circle only asks for a diameter, while the others ask for width and height (or base and height, for a triangle).

2. Enter your dimensions

Type your measurements into the width/height (or diameter) fields, in inches. The cut preview updates live as you type, showing a scaled technical drawing with dimension lines — so you can double check the shape and size look right before going further. A rough estimate is fine for now — you can always confirm exact measurements with a showroom before we cut.

3. Pick your material

On the glass configurator, choose between Clear Tempered, Frosted Tempered, Low-Iron, or Laminated Safety Glass — see our glass types guide for what each is best suited to. On the mirror configurator, choose between Silver, Guardian Glass (Thailand), Grey, Bronze, or Gold mirror — see our mirror tints guide.

4. Choose thickness and edge finish

Thickness affects both price and rigidity — thicker panels are better suited to larger sizes. Edge finish is a cosmetic and safety choice, and works the same way whether you're ordering glass or a mirror: a raw edge is the cheapest but sharp, while a polished or beveled edge costs more but is smooth to the touch and looks more finished. See our edge finishes guide for the full breakdown.

5. Add corner holes and quantity, if needed

If your panel needs to be mounted with visible screws or standoffs, add the number of corner holes you need (up to 4). If you're ordering more than one identical piece, increase the quantity — orders of 5 or more, and 10 or more, automatically receive a volume discount.

6. Review your price breakdown

The price panel on the right shows exactly what you're paying for: material cost, edge finish cost, corner holes, and any volume discount — all the way down to the estimated total. This is a real-time estimate, not a placeholder number.

7. Send your quote to a store

You'll see three WhatsApp buttons, one for each of our showrooms, staying visible throughout — they update live as you adjust the configuration. Once you're happy with your spec, just tap whichever store you'd like to send it to. It opens WhatsApp with your full spec and price pre-filled as a message. Just hit send, and our team will confirm final pricing and next steps with you directly.

That's it — no account, no waiting for a callback. If anything looks off or you're unsure about a measurement, our team can help you double-check it once you reach out.
